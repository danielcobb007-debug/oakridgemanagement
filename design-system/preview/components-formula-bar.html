CEH™ — Draft Framework

CEH = energy consumed per unit of compute output over time.

CEH  =  ( TDP_W × Units × Utilization × PUE ) ÷ 1,000 ÷ Output_Units / hr CEH · Cost  =  CEH × $/kWh (cost basis) CEH · Carbon  =  CEH × kg CO₂ / kWh
TDPHardware power-draw assumption.
UtilizationEstimated operating load.
PUEInfrastructure efficiency factor.
Output Units/hourCompute output normalization (tokens, FLOPs, frames).
Cost BasisEnergy cost applied to the CEH value.
Carbon FactorEmissions intensity of the applied energy source.

Disclosure. Values shown are illustrative and subject to methodology validation. CEH™ is a developing framework and should not be presented as a certified public index or externally adopted benchmark unless separately established.
