Track 03 · Research Partners
Co-Publish the Research
For:
Standards bodies
University labs
Industry analysts
MLCommons · Green Grid
IEEE · ASHRAE

CEH™ methodology is open, versioned, and dated — designed from the outset for independent replication and co-publication. The formula, COU definitions, grade scale, and disclosure checklist are all public and unrestricted.

We invite standards bodies with established standing in IEEE, MLCommons, Green Grid, or ASHRAE to co-author a standards body submission. Academic labs are invited to replicate the index and publish results. Industry analysts are invited to license the CEH™ Index for inclusion in research reports.

  • Full methodology package provided to reviewers on request
  • Co-submission, replication, and analyst licensing tracks available
  • arXiv preprint co-author consideration for replication contributors
