## Prohibited Changes

Claude must not:

- change canonical tags without explicit instruction
- change route ownership
- rename files
- migrate routes
- alter CEH Standard into CEH Index positioning
- add commercialization or capital-stack framing
- introduce DGE&I as a core narrative layer
- alter navigation in ways that change topology assumptions
- create new cross-domain ownership claims
- modify mixed-surface or governed routes outside this scope

Out-of-scope routes include:

- `/ceh-index`
- `/pages/benchmark-index`
- `/pages/partners`
- `/capital-stack`

---

## Escalation Triggers

Claude should stop and escalate if:

- a change appears to require route restructuring
- a change appears to affect canonical ownership
- a change appears to blur CEH Standard and CEH Index
- a change appears to require touching a non-Class-A route
- a change appears to introduce commercialization-sensitive framing
- a change appears to require navigation or deployment changes outside page-level implementation
