Citation Guide

When citing CEH™, the standard, or the index in academic papers, regulatory filings, or industry reports, use one of the following formats. For co-publication inquiries, see the Research Partners track.

APA Format
Cobb, D. (2026). CEH™: A proposed standard unit of measurement for compute energy intensity. DGE&I / Oak Ridge Management. https://oakridgemanagement.net/standard/methodology/
Chicago / Notes-Bibliography
Cobb, Daniel. CEH™: A Proposed Standard Unit of Measurement for Compute Energy Intensity. Atlanta: DGE&I / Oak Ridge Management, 2026. https://oakridgemanagement.net/standard/methodology/
IEEE Format
D. Cobb, "CEH™: A Proposed Standard Unit of Measurement for Compute Energy Intensity," DGE&I / Oak Ridge Management, Atlanta, GA, Apr. 2026. [Online]. Available: https://oakridgemanagement.net/standard/methodology/
BibTeX
@techreport{cobb2026ceh, author = {Cobb, Daniel}, title = {CEH™: A Proposed Standard Unit of Measurement for Compute Energy Intensity}, institution = {DGE\&I / Oak Ridge Management}, year = {2026}, month = apr, address = {Atlanta, GA}, url = {https://oakridgemanagement.net/standard/methodology/} }
