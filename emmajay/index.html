How It Works

Four steps. That's the whole thing.

No subscriptions. No shipping windows. No surprise cleaning bills. Just you, the dress, and the night.

01

Book

Pick a fitting slot through the booking page. A 50% deposit holds your date and reserves the styles you want to try.

02

Try On

A private appointment at the studio. Try on as many dresses as you want, styled with accessories, no rush.

03

Wear It

Take your dress for a 3-day rental window — formal, brunch, photos, after-party. It's yours for the weekend.

04

Drop & Done

Drop the dress back at the studio. We handle the dry cleaning, the steaming, the rotation. You're done.

About

Built by a UGA student. For UGA students.

Hi — I'm Emma, but most people call me EmmaJay. I'm an advertising student at the University of Georgia, and I started Hedge & Hem because every formal season I watched friends spend hundreds on dresses they wore once.

So I built the boutique I wished existed: designer pieces, a private fitting, dry cleaning handled, and a price that doesn't end your weekend before it starts. Sourced for Athens. Styled for Athens. Lived in by Athens.

Welcome to the closet. Stay as long as you'd like.
