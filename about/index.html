Mission

Oak Ridge Management was founded to establish a neutral, auditable standard for measuring compute energy efficiency — and to build and fund the infrastructure that demonstrates what superior CEH™ performance looks like in practice. The two activities are distinct but related: the standard is the measurement framework; DGEI is the first deployment that applies it at commercial scale.

The firm's approach to the CEH™ standard mirrors the model of standards bodies that have successfully created durable measurement infrastructure for adjacent industries — Green Grid (PUE), MLCommons (MLPerf), ASHRAE (data center thermal guidelines) — where a relatively small originating institution establishes a methodology that the broader industry validates, adopts, and eventually submits to formal standards bodies.

That is the explicit goal for CEH™. The standard is open, the methodology is public, and independent replication is invited. Oak Ridge Management's role is to maintain the institutional home, manage the versioning process, and coordinate the co-publication and standards body submission pipeline.
