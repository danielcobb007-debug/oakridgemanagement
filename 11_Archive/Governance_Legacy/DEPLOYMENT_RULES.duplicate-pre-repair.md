# BRAND RULES

## Parent Brand

Oak Ridge Management (ORM) is the parent platform brand and governance authority.

ORM represents:
- platform control
- governance
- commercialization
- strategic architecture
- standards ownership
- AI systems governance

ORM is NOT:
- a consulting firm
- a generic holding company
- subordinate to DGE&I

## CEH Standard

CEH is an ORM-owned standard and intellectual property framework.

CEH represents:
- compute-energy measurement
- benchmarking methodology
- infrastructure standardization
- future institutional market utility

CEH should feel:
- authoritative
- institutional
- precise
- scalable

Avoid:
- hype language
- startup phrasing
- crypto-style branding
- exaggerated claims

## CEH Infrastructure Index

The CEH Index represents:
- benchmarking
- scoring
- analytics
- market intelligence
- future monetization platform

The Index should feel:
- data-driven
- institutional
- premium
- subscription-grade

## DGE&I

DGE&I is:
- deployment partner
- infrastructure execution layer
- reference implementation

DGE&I is NOT:
- the parent ecosystem
- owner of CEH
- governance authority

All DGE&I references should reinforce ORM hierarchy.

## Tone Standards

Preferred tone:
- institutional
- authoritative
- commercially intelligent
- strategic
- concise
- scalable

Avoid:
- consulting jargon
- excessive abstraction
- motivational language
- startup hype
- vague futurism

## Narrative Hierarchy

Correct order:
1. ORM
2. CEH Standard
3. CEH Index
4. DGE&I

Public-facing materials should reinforce this structure consistently.

## Design Direction

Visual direction should feel:
- institutional infrastructure
- modern energy
- premium technology
- capital markets
- systems architecture

Avoid:
- overly futuristic aesthetics
- gaming/cyberpunk visuals
- loud startup branding
- excessive animation

## Governance Rule

No website, pitch deck, partner page, or AI-generated content should contradict these brand rules.
